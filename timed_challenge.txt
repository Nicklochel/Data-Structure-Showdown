These exercises are grouped by data structure logic. Group 1 (Ordered and Indexed) includes tasks like rotating values, tracking running totals with resets, removing duplicates while keeping order, and removing every k-th value. Group 2 (Fast Lookup/Uniqueness) covers distinct word counting, symmetric differences, identifying the first repeated value, and tracking already-seen users. Group 3 (Pointer-Based Traversal) involves inserting after a target, removing by value, reversing a sequence via pointers, and counting nodes. Group 4 (Last-In, First-Out Logic) focuses on balanced symbols, simulating navigation with back/forward, reversing characters, and designing a minimum-retrieving system. Group 5 (First-In, First-Out Logic) includes service line simulation, keeping the last X values, alternating merges, and handling expiring items with timestamps.
